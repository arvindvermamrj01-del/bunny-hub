Copyright & Content Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

BunnyHub expects every person who uploads or publishes content to have the rights and permissions needed to share it. The platform does not authorise users to distribute copyrighted material without permission.

1. What to report

Report a movie, episode, short, song, poster, trailer, subtitle or external media link that you believe infringes copyright, trademark, privacy or another legal right.

2. Information to include

  • Your name and a way for the administrators to contact you.
  • The exact BunnyHub page, title or media URL being reported.
  • A description of the copyrighted work or right you own or represent.
  • A statement explaining why the material is unauthorised and any supporting ownership or authorisation information.
  • A good-faith statement that the information in your report is accurate.

3. How to submit a report

Use the Community Feedback page and choose the complaint/report option. Include “Copyright report” at the start of your submission and provide the information listed above. Do not include passwords, payment PINs or one-time codes.

4. Review and action

Administrators may temporarily restrict access to reported material while reviewing a complaint. If a report is substantiated, the content or link may be removed and the account responsible may be restricted. We may request additional information where necessary.

5. Counter-notices and questions

If you believe material was removed in error, submit a follow-up through Community Feedback with the affected title, the reason you believe you are authorised to publish it, and supporting information.
