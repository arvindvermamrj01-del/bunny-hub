☁️ Google Drive Direct Import

Import Media from Google Drive

Add an authorised, publicly shared Google Drive video to the catalog. The original file stays in Drive; it is not copied into B2.

Setup required: enable Google Drive API in Google Cloud and add GOOGLE_DRIVE_API_KEY as a Cloudflare Pages secret (restrict it to the Drive API; never expose it in browser code). The video must be set to Anyone with the link · Viewer. Import only videos you own or are licensed to publish. Individual video files only; folders and private Drive files are not supported by this link-based connector.

Provider availability

Backblaze B2: Active private S3-compatible upload, signed playback access and server-side deletion for Movie, Series, Short and Song audio files. Requires the B2 secrets in Cloudflare Pages.

Cloudflare R2: Direct S3-compatible upload, signed playback and deletion are supported for media and song audio when R2 secrets are configured.

Supabase: Retained for database, authentication and legacy private media.

Google Drive: Public shared video-file verification and catalog linking when GOOGLE_DRIVE_API_KEY is configured.

PixelDrain / Telegram: Supported via API key (up to 90 MiB) or authorised channel/post links.

Privacy Policy

How BunnyHub handles account, usage and donation information.

Information we handle

When you create an account, Supabase Authentication processes your login credentials. BunnyHub may store your profile name, role, watchlist, viewing progress, content requests, feedback and donation references that you submit.

Usage data and local storage

BunnyHub records limited first-party usage events such as page views, content-detail views, play clicks and download clicks to understand site performance and popular content. Theme preference and basic interface state may be stored in your browser's local storage.

Service providers

The platform may use Supabase for authentication and database services, Cloudflare for hosting and APIs, Backblaze B2 or Cloudflare R2 for private media storage, Google services for authorised Drive imports and advertising, and Telegram for optional bot and channel notifications. Those providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Advertising

If Google AdSense is enabled after approval, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, subject to the consent and privacy requirements that apply in a visitor's region. Advertising is disabled until the site owner configures the required account settings.

Security and retention

Where private storage is used, media is stored in non-public buckets and accessed through signed links; externally supplied URLs remain subject to their provider's access controls. Access to admin functions is role-controlled. Account and platform records are retained as needed to operate the service, protect it from abuse and meet applicable legal obligations.

Your choices

You can manage your account, update your profile, change your password or request account assistance through the site. Do not submit passwords, payment credentials or private API keys in requests or feedback.

For questions, use the official BunnyHub Telegram channel. This policy is a platform information notice and should be reviewed by the site owner for local legal requirements before advertising is enabled.

Requests, feedback and donations
