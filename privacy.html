Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains how BunnyHub handles information when you browse the website, create an account, use its media features, submit feedback or support the platform.

1. Information we handle

  • Account information: email address, display name, account identifier and access role used for sign-in, account security and profile features.
  • Activity and preferences: watchlist items, playback progress, theme preference, content requests and actions needed to operate the website.
  • Site analytics: event type (such as page view, content view or download-link click), content identifier, a random session tag and timestamp. The first-party content analytics table is separate from the security activity log.
  • Security and login activity: Cloudflare's server-provided source IP address, server timestamp, event type (guest visit, user or staff sign-in, failed Admin attempt, blocked attempt or protected-area access), verified account identifier/email and role where available, browser user-agent and page path. This is used to protect accounts and investigate unauthorised access. Raw IP security records are retained for up to 90 days and are viewable in the website's Control Center only by the Main Admin. Passwords, one-time codes and session tokens are not recorded in this activity log.
  • Community submissions: feedback, suggestions, complaints, bug reports, votes and any information you include in them.
  • Donation confirmations: donor name, amount, currency, UPI transaction reference and verification status. UPI payments are completed in your payment app; BunnyHub does not ask for or store your UPI PIN, OTP, card PIN or bank password.
  • Technical information: your browser and device may send IP address, request, security and diagnostic information to our hosting, authentication, storage and advertising providers as part of delivering the service.

2. How we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts, operate profiles and watchlists, remember playback progress, process content requests, moderate community submissions, verify voluntary donations, protect the service, understand aggregate content usage and maintain the website.

3. Storage and service providers

BunnyHub uses Supabase for authentication, database and related account services; Cloudflare for website hosting and server-side functions; and Backblaze B2 or Cloudflare R2 for private media storage where configured. Publicly shared Google Drive links may be used for catalog entries. Telegram may be used for channel announcements and bot notifications when enabled. These providers process information as needed to provide their services and under their own privacy terms.

4. Cookies, local storage and advertising

BunnyHub uses browser storage to maintain selected preferences such as your appearance theme and, where enabled by the authentication client, your sign-in session. If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers to serve, measure and improve advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this and/or other sites. Third parties may place and read cookies in your browser as a result of ad serving on this website.

Google explains its use of information on partner sites in its How Google uses information from sites and apps notice. You can manage personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings or visit aboutads.info for additional choices. Your browser also provides controls for cookies and site storage.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared with service providers only as needed to operate the website, with administrators when necessary to manage requests or verify donations, or when disclosure is required to protect rights, safety or comply with applicable law. Public community posts and published catalog information may be visible to other visitors.

6. Retention and requests

We keep information for as long as it is needed to operate the service, maintain account security, administer content and donations, or meet applicable obligations. You may submit a request to access, correct or delete account-related information through the website's community feedback page. Some records may need to be retained for security, fraud prevention or legal reasons.

7. Security

We use access controls, role-based administration, private media buckets and short-lived media links where supported. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your password private and never share payment PINs or one-time passwords.

8. Children

BunnyHub is not designed for children under 13. Do not create an account or submit personal information if you are under 13.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when features, providers or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
